About

A Dutch preclinical company.

Radigene B.V. develops imaging for engineered cell therapies. Its approach has been shown in mice and is not used in patients.

What we do

Engineered cell therapies, such as CAR-T cells, are living medicines. Once infused, it is hard to see where they go. Radigene is developing a reporter gene and a PET tracer so that engineered cells can be imaged.
