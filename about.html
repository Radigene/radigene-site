About Radigene

Radigene B.V. is a Dutch company, incorporated in 2026. It is preclinical: its imaging approach has been shown in mice and is not used in patients.

What we do

Engineered cell therapies, such as CAR-T cells, are living medicines. Once infused, it is hard to see where they go in the body. Radigene is developing a reporter gene and a PET tracer so that engineered cells can be imaged.
