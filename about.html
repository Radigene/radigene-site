What we do

Engineered cell therapies, such as CAR-T cells, are living medicines. Once infused, it is hard to see where they go. Radigene is developing a reporter gene and a radiolabelled tracer so that engineered cells can be imaged with PET and SPECT, across the lifecycle of a living drug: discovery, development and clinical use.
